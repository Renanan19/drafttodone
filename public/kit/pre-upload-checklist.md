# The pre-upload review checklist

The one that keeps accounts alive. Run it on every book, every time, before you hit publish.
Twenty minutes here is cheaper than losing a catalog.

From the [KDP Operator's Playbook](https://drafttodone.io/en/kdp-operator-playbook).

---

## 1. Compliance — the section that ends catalogs

- [ ] **AI use is declared** where KDP asks for it, and the answer is honest about
      *generated* vs *assisted*. The declaration is not a penalty. Failing to declare is.
- [ ] **The pen name reads like a real person.** No puns, no jokes, no name that could be read
      as impersonating an existing author. This is the single mistake that cost me an account.
- [ ] **The pen name is not already a known author** in any category. Search it on Amazon before
      you commit to it.
- [ ] **Today's upload count is under the daily title limit.** Bursts look exactly like what the
      system is built to catch.
- [ ] **No duplicate or near-duplicate content** against anything else in your catalog. Two books
      that share a chapter are two books at risk, not one.
- [ ] **No content that implies professional advice** you are not qualified to give — medical,
      legal, financial — without a clear disclaimer.
- [ ] **No trademarked terms** in the title, subtitle or keywords.

## 2. The manuscript — read it, don't skim it

You are not reading for pleasure. You are reading for the one paragraph that gets the book
rejected, and the one that gets it refunded.

- [ ] **Opened and read end to end.** Yes, all of it. This is the step everyone skips and it is
      the step that decides whether the book survives.
- [ ] **The promise on the cover is actually delivered** in the chapters. A reader who feels
      misled leaves a 1-star review, and 1-star reviews on a new title are very hard to recover from.
- [ ] **No repeated sections.** Long generated manuscripts drift and restate. Search for repeated
      opening phrases.
- [ ] **No placeholder text** — no `[insert]`, no `TODO`, no `As an AI`.
- [ ] **No invented facts presented as verified** — statistics, studies, quotes, prices, dates.
      If it names a number, either verify it or cut it.
- [ ] **No invented sources.** Fabricated references are the fastest way to a credibility
      complaint. Delete any citation you have not opened.
- [ ] **Chapter count and length match what the description claims.**
- [ ] **Front and back matter present**: title page, copyright page, table of contents.

## 3. Formatting

- [ ] **Trim size matches the interior** you generated.
- [ ] **Margins and gutter** clear the bleed for the page count.
- [ ] **Table of contents links work** in the ebook file.
- [ ] **No orphaned headings** at the bottom of a page.
- [ ] **Page count is final** before the cover spine is generated. Spine width is a function of
      page count; change one and the cover is wrong.

## 4. The cover

- [ ] **Spine width matches the final page count.**
- [ ] **Text is inside the safe zone**, not near the trim edge.
- [ ] **Readable as a thumbnail.** Shrink it to 100px wide. If the title is unreadable, the cover
      has failed — that is the size most buyers actually see.
- [ ] **The back cover copy matches the description** on the listing.
- [ ] **No text baked into the image** that duplicates the title badly, and no garbled
      pseudo-text in the artwork. Zoom in and check.
- [ ] **The genre is legible from the cover alone.** A buyer decides what kind of book this is in
      under a second.

## 5. Metadata — where the ranking is decided

- [ ] **Title carries the promise; subtitle carries the keywords.** Not both in the title.
- [ ] **The first two lines of the description state the outcome**, because that is all most
      buyers read before clicking "read more".
- [ ] **All 7 keyword slots used**, each a distinct search *phrase* a buyer would type.
- [ ] **No keyword repeated** across the title, subtitle and slots. Repetition buys nothing and
      wastes the slot.
- [ ] **No keyword stuffing** in the description.
- [ ] **Two categories chosen that you can realistically rank in**, not the two largest.
- [ ] **Price checked against the royalty band** you intend to be in.
- [ ] **Page count checked against printing cost** so the paperback is not sold at a loss.

## 6. Before you close the tab

- [ ] **Logged in the catalog sheet**: niche, price, category, publish date.
- [ ] **A reminder set for 30 days** to record sales against this book.

---

## If something is wrong after publishing

Fix it and resubmit rather than deleting and re-uploading. A deleted-and-republished title
loses its review history and its ranking, and repeated delete/republish cycles are themselves
a pattern the system notices.

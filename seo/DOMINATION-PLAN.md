# DraftToDone SEO / GEO Domination Plan

Updated: 2026-07-04

This is the legal growth plan for making DraftToDone the cited source for AI
book publishing, KDP book generation and catalog publishing workflows. It avoids
fake ratings, paid link schemes, scraped outreach spam and unverifiable claims.

## North Star

Win three demand layers:

1. **Commercial intent:** people searching for an AI book generator, KDP book
   generator, AI publishing software or competitor alternative.
2. **Problem intent:** people learning how to write, package, price, launch and
   optimize KDP books.
3. **Answer-engine intent:** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Bing and Google AI
   need a clean, citable source for what DraftToDone is and when it fits.

## Already Shipped

- 4-language static site with hreflang.
- Blog moat: 25 pillar guides x 4 locales.
- Commercial pages for tools, workflow and competitor alternatives.
- Buyer-intent pages for `best AI book generator` and `KDP book generator`.
- JSON-LD entity graph for Organization, WebSite and SoftwareApplication.
- `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `ai.txt`, `content-index.json`,
  `answer-engine.json`.
- IndexNow post-deploy notification from the live sitemap.
- No fake `aggregateRating`; ratings wait for real, visible user reviews.

## Weekly Execution Loop

### 1. Search Console and Bing Webmaster

- Submit `https://drafttodone.io/sitemap.xml`.
- Inspect the 20 highest-impression queries with positions 8-30.
- For each query, decide: title rewrite, intro rewrite, internal links, FAQ, or
  new page.
- Track organic clicks to `app.drafttodone.io` as the conversion metric.

### 2. Link Earning

Target legitimate mentions only:

- Indie maker directories: Product Hunt, Betalist, Uneed, Startup Fame, Tiny
  Startups, Indie Hackers launch post.
- AI tool directories with editorial review and dofollow/no-paywall standards.
- Self-publishing resources: KDP blogs, author tool lists, writing workflow
  newsletters, podcast resource pages.
- HARO/Qwoted-style answers where Antoine can comment on AI publishing, KDP
  compliance, AI disclosure, book metadata or catalog operations.

Reject:

- Paid guest-post networks.
- PBN links.
- Auto-generated directory blasts.
- Fake reviews or star ratings.
- Competitor-name spam in KDP metadata or misleading claims.

### 3. Proof Assets

Add these as soon as they are real:

- 3 short customer quotes with name/role permission.
- 1 public case study: brief -> manuscript -> cover -> metadata workflow.
- 1 screenshot/video of the app generating a KDP package.
- Real review count and `aggregateRating` only after reviews are visible on a
  trustworthy public surface.

### 4. Content Expansion

Highest-priority legal pages:

- `best AI publishing software`
- `AI book generator for nonfiction`
- `AI book generator for low-content books`
- `Kindle book generator`
- `ChatGPT for KDP books` as an educational page, not a misleading competitor
  attack.
- More `[competitor] alternative` pages only after verifying the competitor's
  public positioning, pricing and feature scope.

### 5. Distribution

Turn each pillar into distribution:

- 1 LinkedIn/X thread.
- 1 Reddit-safe answer where it genuinely helps; avoid link dropping.
- 1 short YouTube/TikTok script.
- 1 founder note on Indie Hackers or relevant maker communities.

# AI book generators for Amazon KDP: 7 tools, sorted by the job each one does (2026)

*By Antoine, who builds one of them. Prices and features read on each vendor's own site on 11 November 2026; check them again before you buy.*

I ran a KDP catalog to €400 a month and lost the whole account overnight over a pen name that
read like a joke. Since then I have rebuilt my own tooling and looked closely at what else is on
the market.

**My bias, up front:** DraftToDone is mine. It is in this list where it belongs, with the cases
where it is the wrong choice. Discount what I say accordingly and try the others.

## What most "best AI book generator" lists get wrong

Publishing a book on KDP is five jobs, not one:

1. **Choosing the niche.** Judgement. No tool does it for you.
2. **Writing the manuscript.**
3. **Laying out the interior** as a print-ready PDF.
4. **Producing the cover**: front, spine sized to the page count, back.
5. **Writing the listing**: title, subtitle, description, 7 keywords, categories.

Most tools do one or two of these. That is fine for one book. For a catalog it means several
subscriptions and assembling the pieces by hand, every time. So this list is sorted by which
jobs each tool covers.

## The whole-package tools

### KDP Builder: the broadest range of book types

Non-fiction, novels, children's books, puzzle books, coloring books, planners, cookbooks. The
AI drafts each chapter and you edit, approve and lock it as you go. The download includes a
formatted interior, covers for ebook, paperback and hardcover, the description, 7 keywords,
categories and an upload checklist.

- **Price:** credits by subscription. Starter is $16/month for 250 credits, and the site puts a
  non-fiction book at about 150 credits. 75 free credits at signup, which expire after 14 days.
- **Use it if** you publish puzzle, coloring or low-content books, or you want to approve every
  chapter as it is written.
- **Don't if** you want to pay per book with no subscription.

### DraftToDone: one sentence in, the whole book out (this one is mine)

I built it after the ban. The part that ate my time was never the writing. It was reassembling
four separate outputs by hand for every title.

You write one or two sentences: who the book is for and what it promises them. A free preview
shows the title, the promise, an invented pen name and the cover in about a minute. If you like
it, the whole book arrives in about 30 minutes (20 to 45): an editable Word manuscript, a 6×9
interior PDF, the front cover plus the full KDP wrap PDF, and the listing.

You can leaf through real generated books, shown as generated, at
[drafttodone.io](https://drafttodone.io/#sample).

- **Price:** €10 a book with no subscription, or €14.99 a week for 2 books. A credit is spent
  only when the book is finished.
- **Where it is the wrong choice:**
  - **You are writing one book you care deeply about.** This is built for repeatability, not
    for craft. Sudowrite suits that person better.
  - **You want to publish without reading the output.** Nothing removes that step. Long
    generated manuscripts restate themselves and can invent a statistic. Read the book.
  - **You want to steer each chapter.** It writes the whole book from the brief. KDP Builder
    lets you approve chapter by chapter.
  - **You publish puzzle or coloring books.** It does not make them.
  - **You need another language.** Books are written in English or French.
- **Use it if** you publish non-fiction titles regularly and want each one to arrive complete.

## The manuscript tools

### Inkfluence AI: the cheapest way to start writing

Writes by chapters, with a free plan (5 chapters, then 5 more each month, PDF export, a basic
cover designer). Paid plans add EPUB export and audiobook chapters.

- **Price:** free, Creator at $9.99/month, Premium at $19.99/month.
- **Use it if** you want to try AI book writing for nothing, or you want an audiobook.
- **Don't if** you need the KDP listing and a wrap cover sized to your page count in the same
  package: check what your plan covers first.

### Youbooks: long non-fiction, text only

A non-fiction generator that goes up to 300,000 words and can take your own sources into
account. It exports DOCX, EPUB, Markdown or RTF. The site says plainly that generated books do
not include a cover.

- **Price:** from €9.97/month for 200,000 credits; drafting costs about one credit per word.
- **Use it if** you want a long, source-based manuscript and you already have a cover and
  layout workflow.
- **Don't if** you want files you can upload to KDP as they are.

### Sudowrite: the craft tool for fiction

A writing assistant for novelists: description, story structure, character work. It helps you
write; it does not hand you a finished book.

- **Price:** $10, $22 or $44 a month, with a free trial.
- **Use it if** you are writing a novel and the prose is the point.
- **Don't if** you are producing non-fiction titles regularly: it stops at the manuscript.

## The single-job tools that are worth having anyway

### Atticus: formatting, and only formatting

Often listed among AI book tools, and it is not one. You bring a finished manuscript and it
produces clean interiors and ebooks. $147 once, updates included.

### Publisher Rocket: keyword and category research

Not a writing tool. It researches Amazon keywords, categories and competitors. $199 once.
If you take one thing from this page: **the listing decides whether anyone sees your book; the
manuscript decides whether they keep it.**

## How to choose

| Your situation | The tool |
|---|---|
| Puzzle, coloring, low-content or children's books | KDP Builder |
| Non-fiction titles, regularly, each one complete from one brief | DraftToDone |
| Trying AI book writing for free, or you want an audiobook | Inkfluence AI |
| A long manuscript built on your own sources | Youbooks |
| One novel, and you care about the prose | Sudowrite |
| Finished manuscripts that need clean interiors | Atticus |
| Serious keyword and category research | Publisher Rocket |

Most publishers end up with two or three of these. Anyone telling you one tool replaces the
whole stack is selling you something, and that includes me.

## What no tool on this list does

None of them chooses your niche, and none of them reads the book before you upload it. Those
two steps are where accounts get terminated and where catalogs quietly fail. Amazon also
requires you to declare AI-generated content when you publish; that declaration is yours to
make, whichever tool wrote the text.

# Press assets — the ban story

The one asset this site has that no competitor can copy: a founder who ran an
Amazon KDP catalog, was permanently banned over a pen-name issue, and rebuilt
the automation into a product. It is true, it is verifiable, and it is the kind
of story the self-publishing press actually covers.

**Why this and not more content:** DR 0, 8 referring domains, no deep editorial
link to any page. One placement on a DR-60 self-publishing site outweighs the
entire 175-page site for ranking purposes, and the pitches below are the
cheapest way to get one.

## Before sending — three things to fill in

The pitches are written to be sent as they are, except for three facts only
Antoine has. Each is marked `[[ ]]` in the files:

| Placeholder | What it needs |
|---|---|
| `[[MONTH YEAR]]` | when the account was terminated |
| `[[N]]` | how many titles were live in the catalog at the time |
| `[[APPEAL OUTCOME]]` | what Amazon said on appeal, in one clause |

**Do not guess these.** A press pitch that gets a detail wrong is worse than no
pitch, and a journalist who checks one number checks the rest.

## Rules that apply to every file here

- Sign **Antoine** only. Never the surname.
- No claim about rankings, income guarantees or KDP approval.
- No invented numbers. The €400/month catalog figure and the permanent ban are
  the only revenue and status facts on record; everything else stays vague or
  gets filled in above.
- The product mention goes **last** and stays one sentence. A pitch that leads
  with the product is an ad, and editors bin ads.

## Order to send

1. **r/selfpublishing** first — free, fast, and the response tells you whether
   the story lands before you spend an editor's attention on it. Post it as a
   post, not a plug; the sub removes promotion.
2. **The Creative Penn** and **Written Word Media** next, in parallel. Both run
   guest and interview formats about exactly this.
3. **ALLi** last — the slowest to respond, and the strongest link if it lands.

# Alliance of Independent Authors (ALLi) — pitch

**Target:** the ALLi Self-Publishing Advice blog / Watchdog desk.
**Format they run:** member-facing advice, and the Watchdog service that tracks
how platforms and services treat indie authors.
**Angle:** a documented account closure, offered as a case study rather than a
complaint. ALLi's Watchdog exists to collect exactly this.
**Why last:** slowest to respond of the three, and the strongest link if it
lands, because ALLi pages get cited by other advice sites for years.

**Subject line:** Case study for the Watchdog desk: permanent KDP closure over a
pen name

---

Hello,

I am writing to offer a documented case rather than to ask for help — the
outcome is settled and I am not appealing further.

In [[MONTH YEAR]] Amazon permanently closed my KDP account over a pen-name
issue. [[N]] titles were live; the catalog earned around €400 a month. On appeal,
[[APPEAL OUTCOME]]. I still have the closure notice, the appeal correspondence
and the dates.

Two ways this might be useful to ALLi:

1. **As a Watchdog data point.** If you track closure reasons and appeal
   outcomes, mine is a clean, dated example of the pen-name category, and I am
   willing to have it recorded with or without my name on it.

2. **As an advice piece for members**, if you want one. The gap I keep hitting
   is that the community's understanding of pen names on KDP and the actual
   wording of the terms are not the same thing, and nobody finds out which is
   which until an account closes. I would write it straight: what I did, what
   the documentation says, what the appeal process does and does not allow you
   to argue, and what a member should check tonight.

Disclosure: I now build a small tool for publishing workflows. I am not asking
for it to be mentioned or linked, and I will sign whatever conflict declaration
you use for contributors.

Antoine
contact@drafttodone.io

---

## Notes for sending

- ALLi has a members-only layer; a non-member contribution is possible but
  membership is worth checking first, as it changes which desks will respond.
- Lead with the Watchdog offer, not the guest post. Their brief is collecting
  platform-behaviour evidence, and a case study costs an editor nothing to
  accept.
- Offering the piece anonymously is deliberate: it makes the offer about their
  readers rather than about a backlink, and it is what makes the offer credible.
  If they accept it anonymously, that is still a win — the story circulating
  matters more than one link.

# The Creative Penn — pitch

**Target:** Joanna Penn's site and podcast (audience: professional and
semi-professional indie authors; long-running, high authority).
**Format they run:** interviews, and guest posts on the business and risk side
of self-publishing. Joanna covers AI in publishing directly and without panic,
which is why this angle fits her and not everyone.
**Angle:** platform risk. One account, one policy decision, everything gone.

**Subject line:** Guest post or interview: losing a KDP account, and what
platform risk really means for indie authors

---

Hi Joanna,

I lost my Amazon KDP account in [[MONTH YEAR]]. Permanent closure over a pen-name
issue, [[N]] live titles, and on appeal [[APPEAL OUTCOME]]. It was a small
catalog — around €400 a month — which is exactly why I think it is worth writing
about: it was small enough that I had never seriously thought about what would
happen if it stopped.

You have talked for years about not building your business on rented land. I had
heard that and had not acted on it. I would like to write the version of that
warning that comes with the receipts.

Two formats, whichever suits you:

**Guest post, ~1,500 words — "The day the account closed."** The sequence as it
happened, what the KDP terms actually say about pen names versus what the
community believes, what I could and could not recover, and the specific things
I now do differently: a mailing list I own, files stored outside the platform,
and a rule about how much of my income any one channel is allowed to be.

**Or an interview**, if the conversation is more useful than the essay. I am
comfortable being asked the uncomfortable version of the question — what did you
do wrong — because the honest answer is the useful part.

One disclosure so it is not a surprise later: I now run a small tool for
publishing workflows. I am not pitching it, I am happy for it to be absent from
the piece entirely, and I will not ask for a link.

Antoine
contact@drafttodone.io

---

## Notes for sending

- The Creative Penn publishes guest-post guidelines; read the current ones
  before sending and match the word count and format she asks for. A pitch that
  ignores stated guidelines gets deleted regardless of the story.
- The "not building on rented land" framing is genuinely hers — it is why this
  pitch fits her site specifically. Do not reuse that paragraph for the other
  outlets; it will read as a template.
- She is measured about AI rather than hostile, so the disclosure line is safe
  to include. Leaving it out and having it surface later is the risk.

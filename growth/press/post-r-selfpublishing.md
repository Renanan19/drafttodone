**The sequence, with timings**

- Closure email arrives. It does not say which title or which rule.
- The appeal form gives you [[X]] characters and no attachments.
- [[Response time]].

- Titles: removed from sale immediately, not on appeal resolution.

**What I do differently now**

1. A mailing list I own, started before the next book, not after.
2. Manuscript, cover files and metadata stored outside the platform.
3. No single channel above a share of income I can afford to lose.
4. Pen names declared consistently across every title and every account field.
5. I read the terms when they change. Amazon emails about it; I used to delete
   those.

**What I would ask anyone reading this**

If you publish under a pen name, open the KDP help page tonight and read the
actual wording rather than the version you remember. It takes four minutes. I
did not do that, and it cost me the catalog.

# Written Word Media — pitch

**Target:** the Written Word Media blog (Publisher Rocket / Freebooksy audience:
working indie authors and small publishers).
**Format they run:** practical, data-led posts and first-person operator stories.
**Angle:** what a pen name actually costs you when Amazon decides it is a
violation — told by someone it happened to.

**Subject line:** Amazon closed my KDP account over a pen name. Here is what I
learned about the rules nobody reads.

---

Hi,

I ran a small KDP catalog until [[MONTH YEAR]], when Amazon permanently closed
my account over a pen-name issue. [[N]] live titles, around €400 a month,
terminated. On appeal, [[APPEAL OUTCOME]].

I have spent the months since reading the parts of the KDP terms that I clearly
had not read carefully enough, and rebuilding my publishing process so the same
thing cannot happen twice. I think there is a piece in it for your readers,
because almost every indie author I talk to uses a pen name and almost none of
them can tell you what Amazon's actual position on them is.

What I would write, roughly 1,400 words:

1. **What I did, stated plainly.** Not a wronged-innocent story — I will say
   exactly what I got wrong.
2. **What the KDP terms actually say about pen names**, quoted, with the help
   pages linked. Most of the advice circulating in Facebook groups contradicts
   the documentation.
3. **The account-closure sequence from the inside** — what the email says, what
   the appeal form allows you to argue, how long each step took, what happens to
   royalties already earned.
4. **The five checks I now run before a title goes live.** This is the part
   readers can act on the same day.
5. **What I would tell someone with a pen name and no backup plan.**

No affiliate links, no pitch inside the piece. I run a publishing tool now and I
am happy for that to be a one-line author bio or to go unmentioned entirely —
your call, and it does not change whether I write it.

I can send a draft within a week of a yes.

Antoine
contact@drafttodone.io

---

## Notes for sending

- Written Word Media takes contributor pitches through their contact form and
  through the editor's address on the blog. Use whichever is current — check the
  site before sending rather than trusting a saved address.
- Keep the offer to drop the product mention entirely. It is what makes this a
  pitch rather than a link request, and the author bio link is the one that
  matters anyway.
- If they say yes: the piece must link to the KDP help pages it quotes. That is
  what makes it useful and what makes it survive an editor.
